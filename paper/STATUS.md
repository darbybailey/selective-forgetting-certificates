# Status

This repository collects restricted positive results and matching barriers for selective forgetting certificates in residual and four-matching models.

- The three-way residual-certificate protocol is verified and scales.
- The 5V/12 affine bound is sharp and attained.
- The uniform Residual Exclusion Problem remains open.

Suitable for a Zenodo working-paper deposit. Priority for the exact statements is left open for independent review.
