# Independent stress controls for exact extremality

The written recognition argument in ../overlap/RECOGNITION.md passes the following additional checks. For mixed components, take disjoint 64-row coordinate cubes and exchange a third point between two matching rows in consecutive cubes. This preserves the four perfect matchings and the indicated original exact grid cover. The checker revalidates pair linearity through the independent tile enumerator and verifies connectivity. For 2, 4 and 10 linked cubes, each under two seeded choices, the raw degree spectra of the footprint cover equations include one, two and three. Global subset deletion retains every chosen old block and propagates across all 128, 256 or 640 rows; these controlled systems finish with one option everywhere. The full 64-row cube control has three options everywhere and performs no elimination.

A repeated-label control is obtained by quotienting the 4 by 4 by 4 coordinate cube by the simultaneous translation (i,j,k) → (i+1,j+1,k+1) in GF(4). Points are the 24 two-coordinate fibers modulo this translation. Taking rows modulo translation leaves 32 triples. Their four matching colors are i+ωj+ω²k with ω²=ω+1; translation preserves the matching index because 1+ω+ω²=0. The checker verifies the matchings, pair linearity and all candidate footprints. There are six footprints and three candidates at every row. In each surviving footprint, parallel sides repeat co-labels. Independent generation of all 192 ordered row states verifies equality of the relations “move with colors a then b” and “move with colors b then a” for all 1,728 state/type pairs. Two equal-type moves also equal one move. Thus the commuting argument survives repeated labels on parallel sides and a nontrivial quotient of the cube.
